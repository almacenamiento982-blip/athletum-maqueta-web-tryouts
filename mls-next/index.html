How to join

By invitation.

MLS NEXT is invitational. Current Athletum and Juventus Academy Miami players are evaluated during the season and do not need to apply.

STEP 01

Apply with a video

Submit your application on PlayMetrics with a video that shows your talent.

STEP 02

Club review

Our staff reviews every application and contacts you by phone or email if we need more information.

STEP 03

ID Camp invitation

Selected players are invited to an in-person ID Camp. If not selected for MLS NEXT, we invite you to the Elite tryouts.
