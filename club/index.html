Our story

From Hialeah to the nation.

Athletum FC was officially launched in Hialeah in March 2023 and joined MLS NEXT that same year. Since the 2026-27 season, every competitive team of the Juventus Academy Miami family plays as Athletum, boys and girls.

Today the club fields 12 MLS NEXT teams in the Homegrown and Academy divisions, competitive teams in four Florida leagues and a girls program in Doral and Cutler Bay.

The Juventus connection

From Miami to Italy.

Athletum is part of the Juventus Academy Miami family. In March 2026, 33 Athletum players traveled to Italy to train and play against Italian academies, including Juventus.

  • Juventus Academy methodology. The same training principles used by the Juventus academies around the world.
  • Juventus Residency. A full season training in Italy, one of the steps of our pathway.
  • Real experience abroad. Matches against Italian academies and training at professional facilities.
