One club. The whole pathway.

From the Pre-Academy to MLS NEXT, and then on to college or the pros. Every player is placed at the level that fits them after the tryout.

  1. Pre-AcademyJuventus Academy Miami methodology for the youngest players.
  2. CompetitiveLeague play in EDP Florida, Florida Academy League, South Florida Premier League and National Academy League.
  3. MLS NEXT · Juventus ResidencyMLS NEXT Homegrown and Academy divisions against the top academies in the country, or a full season training in Italy with Juventus Residency.
  4. MLS NEXT ProThe professional development league, the bridge between the academy and the pros.
  5. College · ProCollege soccer (NCAA, NJCAA, NAIA) and pro or semi-pro soccer around the world.

FAQ

Who can try out?

Boys and girls. Each age group has its own date and time.

When are the tryouts?

The official tryouts are in May 2027. Apply now and we will contact you with the date and location for your age group.

What should my child bring?

A white or black jersey, cleats, shin guards and water. Please arrive 30 minutes early to check in.

What happens after the tryout?

You will receive an invitation letter within a few days to a week with the team and level for your player.

Is Athletum part of Juventus Academy Miami?

Athletum FC is the competitive and MLS NEXT program of the Juventus Academy Miami family.
